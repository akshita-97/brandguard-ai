Workflow: Author submits content → Reviewer reviews findings and submits review → Approver makes final approve/reject decision. Backend enforces role permissions for logged-in users. Anonymous demo mode allows all actions so judges can test the full workflow without registering.
How to test (for judges)
  1. Click Load sample content → Check Content.
  2. See "Needs Review (High Priority)" with findings.
  3. Click Review on any finding → read explanation, rule, citation, suggestion.
  4. Click Accept / Reject / Resolve.
  5. Click Approve content or Reject content.
  6. Go to History → click View → decisions are saved.
  7. Upload an image in Visual brand check → see colour/dimension/format checks.
  8. Register/login → see notifications and analytics.
How it works
  1. Paste content and choose a brand profile.
  2. The rule engine checks prohibited terms, facts, claims, tone and accessibility.
  3. Optional AI adds contextual observations.
  4. Review each finding: accept, reject, or mark resolved.
  5. Record a final human approval or rejection.

The system never auto-publishes. A human makes the final decision.
