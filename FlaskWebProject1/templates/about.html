About BrandGuard AI

A brand-governance platform that checks content before publication.

What it does

BrandGuard AI helps organizations check content against approved brand rules, terminology, and facts before publication. It detects possible issues, explains why they matter, suggests corrections, and requires a human reviewer to make the final decision.

How it works
  1. Paste or type content and select a brand profile.
  2. The rule engine checks for prohibited terms, factual conflicts, unsupported claims, tone, and accessibility issues.
  3. Optional AI adds contextual observations (when configured).
  4. Review each finding: accept, reject, resolve, or mark as needing review.
  5. Record a final human approval or rejection with notes.
Technology

Python Flask backend, SQLite database, deterministic rule engine, optional OpenAI-compatible AI assistance, Bootstrap 5 frontend. Automated tests included.

Demo data

All brand data in the demo profile is fictional and clearly labelled as demo data. No real company policies are represented.
